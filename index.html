Supporting videos

Interfacial breakup and satellite formation in electrowetting-driven droplets: Dynamical thresholds beyond shape-only criteria

Z.T. Wang — School of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering, Zhongshan Polytechnic, Zhongshan, China

Twenty-two movies accompanying the manuscript submitted to Journal of Colloid and Interface Science. S01–S09 and S22 are experimental high-speed records; S10–S21 are computed interface animations for the parameter scans. Recorded frame rates and windows are quoted as acquired; no time stamps are drawn on the frames, so the time axis is given by the stated rate.
